## What this pin adds

From `117c158a545b` to this pin is four commits: that brief, its merge
(#107), and #103 with its merge. Only #103 changes code.

#103 puts a "Search engines" panel at the top of each paper's Settings
page, in place of the old checkbox. It has one button: Open to search
engines, or Hide once a paper is open. Open refuses while any of the
paper's demonstration stories is published or scheduled, or while its
pages name another site's address.
- What counts as demonstration copy is declared in the code: fifteen
  launch packs say `'demonstration' => true`, and the flagship's twelve
  core-seed stories count too.
- `tools/demo-inventory.php` reports the same list.

The roll turns nothing on:
- every paper keeps its own `indexing_enabled`, and every page keeps
  `noindex`;
- no schema step, no route and no server step;
- #103 changes no reader-facing page: its render baseline was
  byte-identical to its base.

D16 and D17 below confirm the switch arrived and nothing opened. They
also report what each paper must draft before its newsroom can press the
button.

- the fixed upgrader;

    test -f "$NEW/app/indexing.php" && grep -c 'id="search-engines"' "$NEW/admin/settings.php"

Expect `1`. Then ask the database which papers are open:

    (cd "$NEW" && php -r 'require "app/bootstrap.php"; $s = db()->prepare("SELECT s.slug FROM settings t JOIN sites s ON s.id = t.site_id WHERE t.skey = ? AND t.svalue = ? ORDER BY s.slug"); $s->execute(["indexing_enabled", "1"]); $r = $s->fetchAll(PDO::FETCH_COLUMN); echo $r ? implode("\n", $r) : "no paper is open to search engines", "\n";')

    while IFS=$'\t' read -r block H code rest; do
