THREE HILLS — After a three-hour debate that filled the gallery, county council voted 5–2 Monday to approve a per-tonne levy on gravel hauled from pits on county land.

The levy, set at 40 cents a tonne, takes effect Jan. 1. Reeve Carol Mackenzie said the money is earmarked for the roads the trucks actually run on.

“The people who wear the road out should help pay for it. That's the whole argument, and it won.”

Two councillors voted against, arguing the levy will be passed straight through to county ratepayers on every yard of concrete poured.
