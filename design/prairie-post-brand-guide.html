01

The idea

A prairie photograph is two shapes: sky and land, divided by one hard line. The Prairie Post is built on that line.

The horizon rule runs under the masthead, under every section head, and under every card. It is the only structural device the brand needs, and it carries meaning — above it, the open field the paper reserves for its own name; below it, the reporting. Everything else stays quiet.

The wordmark borrows from prairie signwriting rather than Fleet Street: heavy condensed capitals, the lettering painted on grain elevators and highway signs across the west. It reads at 300 px on a homepage and at 40 px on a phone, which is where most of the audience will meet it.

Positioning — a regional daily for people who live between the towns: farm and market news alongside council, courts, weather and community. Plain, close to the ground, unhurried, never folksy.

Cloud, lifted

On the red. There is no red anywhere in the photograph. A news brand still needs one signal colour for live coverage, corrections and opinion, so Bin Red is borrowed from the other thing that is always red on a prairie section: the paint on a grain bin and a barn door. It is the only colour in the system without a coordinate, and it is rationed accordingly — never as decoration, only as a signal.

03

Type

Three faces, three jobs, no overlap. All open-licence, all servable from your own domain.

Display — Archivowidth 68 · weight 800 · caps · +2% tracking
Grain elevator

The masthead, section fronts and standing furniture. Archivo is a variable grotesque; running it narrow and heavy gets the painted-sign density without the novelty of a true elevator face. Never set body copy in it, and never let it go below width 65 — past that the counters close.

Body — Newsreaderweight 400 · 19px / 1.62
Council votes 5–2 to keep the grader

Article text, ledes and headlines inside stories. Newsreader was drawn for screen news at reading sizes and holds up in a long scroll where a high-contrast display serif would fall apart. Italics for ship-and-vessel names, publication titles and quoted terms only.

04

Logo

Primary is the wordmark on the rule. The farmstead only appears where the wordmark cannot go wide.

Primary · horizontal
Reversed · on Shelterbelt
Stacked · narrow & print

The mark is the source photograph reduced to five bands: sky, distant hill, the horizon, the field, the stubble in the foreground. Below 32 px the shelterbelt and the second building close up, so the reduced mark drops to one gable and a thicker horizon. Clear space on every lockup is one cap height of the wordmark on all four sides; the rule may bleed past that to the edge of its container, and usually should.

Do

  • Let the horizon rule run full-bleed out of the lockup and across the container.
  • Set the mark on Noon Sky, Cloudbank, or a photograph with a clear horizon.
  • Use the stacked lockup under 360 px and on anything printed narrow.

Don't

  • Rebuild the wordmark in another face, or stretch, arc or outline it.
  • Add a second rule above the wordmark. There is one horizon.
  • Tint the wordmark in Stubble or Bin Red, or place it over the busy part of a photo.
  • Put the farmstead beside the horizontal lockup — it belongs to the mark and the stacked form.

07

Voice

Plain, specific, and close to the ground. The paper is written for people who will recognise the road being described.

Write it this way

  • Name the place in the dateline, always.HANNA — Council spent ninety minutes on one culvert.
  • Use the units people actually use.a quarter section, 850 lb steers, 40 minutes of gravel
  • Say who is speaking, or say why you can't.Two councillors, who asked not to be named because the file is in camera, said…
  • Empty states point at the next thing.No stories filed from Kneehill this week. Try the county council archive.
  • Errors say what broke and what to do.The market feed didn't load. Prices last updated 4:15 p.m. Reload.

Not this way

  • Folksy register. The audience lives here; they don't need it performed back at them.Well folks, out here on the ol' homestead…
  • Metro shorthand for rural distance.“just outside the city” for a place 90 km away
  • Vague attribution used as filler.Sources say · many are asking · some believe
  • Buttons that don't name their outcome.Submit · Learn more · Click here

Tagline. News to the horizon is the standing line: it points at the rule, sets the geographic promise, and stays out of the way. Runners-up worth keeping for campaigns: Everything under this sky and Reported from the road it happened on. The tagline is set in Plex Mono, uppercase, +0.34em, never in the display face.

08

Tokens

Drop this in first. Every value above resolves to one of these.

:root{
  /* Ink & ground */
  --pp-shelterbelt:#17301C;   /* primary ink, masthead, rule */
  --pp-quarter:#3F5A22;       /* agriculture, secondary ink */
  --pp-field:#58651C;         /* charts, data */
  --pp-fencepost:#6F5535;     /* community */
  --pp-stubble:#7A661F;       /* business & markets */
  --pp-binred:#9C3B22;        /* opinion, live, corrections */

  /* Sky & light — fills only below 4.5:1 */
  --pp-bigsky:#2F6C99;        /* links, interactive */
  --pp-noonsky:#77B2D6;       /* weather, large fills */
  --pp-highsky:#A9CDE4;       /* tints, hover beds */
  --pp-straw:#B99A45;         /* highlight fills */
  --pp-board:#C4C0B4;         /* rules, dividers — never text */
  --pp-cloudbank:#F1F2F0;     /* page background */

  /* Type */
  --pp-display:'Archivo','Arial Narrow',sans-serif;
  --pp-body:'Newsreader',Georgia,serif;
  --pp-mono:'IBM Plex Mono',ui-monospace,monospace;
  --pp-display-vf:'wdth' 68,'wght' 800;
  --pp-hed-vf:'wdth' 78,'wght' 700;

  /* Structure */
  --pp-rule:4px solid var(--pp-shelterbelt);
  --pp-hairline:1px solid var(--pp-board);
  --pp-measure:68ch;
  --pp-radius:0;              /* the brand has no rounded corners */
}

Two rules that are easy to lose in a build: the horizon is 4px plus a 1px hairline two pixels below it, not a single border; and radius is zero everywhere, including buttons, cards and images. A rounded corner on this brand reads as a different paper.
